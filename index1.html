What is CBD?

CBD, or cannabidiol, is a naturally occurring compound found in hemp. It is non-intoxicating and is commonly used in wellness products.

Will CBD make me feel high?

CBD itself is not intoxicating. Product effects vary by person, formula, serving size, and whether a product contains trace THC.

What is the difference between hemp and marijuana?

Both are cannabis plants, but hemp is defined by its low THC content. Marijuana generally refers to cannabis bred with higher THC levels.

Are your products lab tested?

Yes. We prioritize products supported by current third-party laboratory reports and clear ingredient information.

Where can I find a certificate of analysis?

Ask our support team for the latest certificate of analysis for a specific batch or product before purchase.

Is CBD legal?

Laws differ by country, state, and product type. Customers are responsible for understanding the rules that apply where they live.

Can I travel with CBD?

Travel rules vary widely, especially across borders. Check the laws of every destination and transit location before traveling.

Do your products contain THC?

Ingredients differ by item. Review each product label and its laboratory report for THC information before ordering.

Are CBD products safe?

Many adults tolerate CBD well, but no product is risk-free. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about personal suitability.

Can CBD interact with medication?

CBD may interact with some medicines. Consult your doctor or pharmacist before use if you take prescription or over-the-counter medication.

Who should avoid CBD?

People who are pregnant, breastfeeding, under the legal purchase age, or managing a medical condition should seek professional advice first.

How old do I need to be to shop?

You must meet the legal age requirement where you live. We may request age verification for regulated products.

How much CBD should I take?

Start with the smallest serving suggested on the label, observe your response, and do not exceed the recommended amount.

How often can I use CBD?

Follow the product label and your healthcare provider's guidance. Keep a simple record of your serving and response.

How long does CBD take to work?

Timing depends on the product format, serving, metabolism, and whether it is taken with food. Individual experiences vary.

How long do CBD effects last?

Duration varies based on the product, serving, metabolism, and personal factors. Avoid combining products without understanding total intake.

Should I take CBD with food?

Some products are best taken as directed on their label. A consistent routine makes it easier to understand your own response.

Can I use CBD in the morning?

Many adults include CBD in morning or evening routines. Choose a format and serving that suits your schedule and needs.

Can CBD help me sleep?

Some people use CBD as part of an evening routine, but we do not make medical claims. Discuss persistent sleep concerns with a clinician.

Can CBD help with stress?

CBD is used by some adults for general wellness. It is not a treatment for anxiety or any medical condition.

What are common side effects?

Some people report tiredness, digestive changes, or changes in appetite. Stop use and seek advice if you experience concerning symptoms.

What should I do if I take too much?

Stop using the product, monitor how you feel, and contact a healthcare professional or poison information service if symptoms concern you.

Are CBD gummies vegan?

Check the individual product page because ingredients vary. Dietary, allergen, and certification details should be reviewed before purchase.

Are your products gluten-free?

Some products may be gluten-free, but customers should confirm the current ingredient and allergen statement for each item.

Do you offer vegan options?

Yes, selected products may suit vegan customers. Review each label and contact us if you need help finding a suitable item.

What is full-spectrum CBD?

Full-spectrum extracts contain multiple naturally occurring hemp compounds and may include legally permitted trace THC.

What is broad-spectrum CBD?

Broad-spectrum products contain multiple hemp compounds and are typically formulated without detectable THC, subject to testing.

What is CBD isolate?

CBD isolate is a purified form of CBD without the other naturally occurring hemp compounds.

What does “entourage effect” mean?

It is a theory that hemp compounds may work together. Research is ongoing, so we avoid promising a specific outcome.

How should I store CBD oil?

Keep it sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, heat, moisture, children, and pets.

How long do CBD products last?

Check the printed best-before date and storage instructions. Do not use products that look, smell, or taste unusual.

Can children use CBD?

Our products are intended for adults. Never give CBD to a child without direct guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.

Can pets use CBD?

Human products are not automatically suitable for animals. Ask a veterinarian before giving any hemp product to a pet.

Do you sell nicotine or tobacco products?

Our available inventory and local rules may change. Contact the shop for current smoke-accessory and regulated-product information.
